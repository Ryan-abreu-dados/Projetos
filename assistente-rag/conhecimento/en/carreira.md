## What kind of role is Ryan looking for?
Ryan is looking for a data-focused internship or a junior data engineering position. In Greater São
Paulo, preferably near Barueri, he accepts on-site, hybrid or remote work. Outside São Paulo, remote only.

## Where does Ryan want to be in 3 to 5 years?
In 3 years, a junior data engineer finishing college. In 5 years, a mid-level data engineer.

## Why data engineering and not another field?
At first Ryan considered data analysis, but he wanted to go further and chose data engineering, which is
the field he enjoys most today. In the future, he also wants to explore data science.

## What does Ryan look for in a company?
Above all, the chance to learn from more experienced professionals. He believes that by working with
people who are very good at what they do, he has a better chance of becoming that kind of professional
too.
