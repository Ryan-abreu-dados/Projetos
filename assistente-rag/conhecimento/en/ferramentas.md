## What tools and languages does Ryan use?
Ryan uses SQL and PostgreSQL, PL/pgSQL, CDC (logical replication), ETL/ELT, Supabase, n8n, TypeScript and
React, Git, Python and Claude Code. He also integrates APIs such as WhatsApp's and the company's financial
system. At work he uses all of these in production, except Python, which he used at Tel Contact Center
and in personal projects.

## How good is Ryan at SQL and Python?
Between basic and intermediate, in both. Ryan is not an expert yet, but he already uses these
technologies to solve real problems at work.

## Has Ryan worked with the cloud (AWS, Azure, GCP)?
Not yet. Ryan plans to study AWS.

## Has Ryan built dashboards?
Yes. Ryan builds dashboards directly in the front end, with React, Vite and a charting library, with data
coming from Supabase. One example is the view of disqualified leads for the sales team.

## What doesn't Ryan know yet, and what is he learning now?
Ryan is starting to study the cloud (AWS). He wants to go deeper into data modeling and architecture: how
to design a table, a login, a complete system such as a CRM well, and what is considered good practice in
each part.
