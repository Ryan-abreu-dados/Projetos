## Who is Ryan?
Ryan Abreu has been a data-focused intern at Almeida e Matos Sociedade de Advogados, a law firm in
Alphaville (Barueri, São Paulo), since May 2026, where he works on data engineering in a real production
environment. He builds data pipelines, incremental replication (CDC) between PostgreSQL databases and
faster queries, focused on reliable data. He finishes his technical degree in Systems Development at
ETEC in Barueri in December 2026.

## What led Ryan to data?
Ryan chose data because, from his research, it is a field that will keep growing a lot: data is more and
more valuable to companies, and whoever collects, stores and uses it well has an edge. He started by
automating tasks as an apprentice and now works on data engineering in his internship. What drives him
is using data to help people find answers.

## What does Ryan enjoy most at work?
Ryan enjoys building data pipelines that answer specific questions and help management change something
for the better. For example: a pipeline that shows which area of the company is getting the best results,
so managers can decide where to invest.

## What does Ryan do outside work?
Outside work, Ryan enjoys playing video games and working out at the gym. He lives in Barueri, São Paulo.
