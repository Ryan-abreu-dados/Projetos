## What did Ryan do at Tel Contact Center?
From April 2024 to December 2025, Ryan was an apprentice at Tel Contact Center, in Alphaville (Barueri,
São Paulo). There he built, on his own initiative, a Python automation that replaced a manual routine of
renaming files in batches: the script standardizes each file name (PDF, JPG, JPEG, JFIF) as Name_Date,
using the delivery date, and builds an Excel spreadsheet with the monthly total using pandas. A manual
task of about 30 minutes now runs in a few seconds.

## Was the file organizer adopted by the company?
Ryan built the organizer for his own work and then showed it to his colleagues, who liked it. It was not
officially adopted, because Ryan was not part of the IT team and the change would have depended on the
company's outsourced IT team.
